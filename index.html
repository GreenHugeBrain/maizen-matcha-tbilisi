Our story

Matcha, done properly.

MAIZEN is a matcha bar built on care. We whisk ceremonial-grade matcha the way it's meant to be — smooth, vivid and grassy-sweet — and pull specialty espresso for those who lean coffee.

It's a calm, minimal corner of Tbilisi to slow down, sip something beautiful, and reset. Hot or iced, oat or dairy, always made with intention.
